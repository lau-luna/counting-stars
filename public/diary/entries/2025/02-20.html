Homesickness

Thursday, 20th February 2025

Previous

Last night I dreamed about my first house, my childhood house. I dreamed about my family; my little siblings, mom and dad. They were there, along with my pets. I lived there until I was like 11, when we moved. So most of my precious memories, and the things I did as a child, are left there inside that walls. In my dream, I was at that old backyard at night, the scenety reminded me of some good old christmas eve we had there, that time my siblings and I recieved a lot of gifts and toys, it was such a pure hapiness. Dreaming, dreaming I was transported to that moment, but with the only difference that I had my current age, 19. It could have been a good dream, sure, but a sense of nostalgia and a crushing sadness hit me. Then, I started nosebleeding inside the dream. I woke up with a blood taste in my mouth, it happened to me before, so I tought I was bleeding again. It didn't it was just the taste. I felt overwhelmed.

It's been more than half a month I'm living in another city for stuying. I'm taking the entace course for computer science I was doing well with the change, but some toughts of loging were acummulating at the bottom of my heart. That moment I realized how much life has changed for me. I turned 19 a few weeks ago, damn. Now I'm living with a friend, but I'm no longer living with with my family. Fuck, I miss home, man. I miss mom, and dad. I miss my siblings, my cousin and uncles, my grandmas and grandpa. I miss my dog and my cats. I miss my bedroom and my instruments. At what point did I become so old? I cried a bit last night. I went to the bathroom and I spitted some blood.

My nose bled a bit when I got up. Then, I had a coffee and went to university. Algorithms class was good, they gave me more excercises because I finished all quickly. I already know these introduction themes. Even my teacher copied one of the functions from my code that draws a five side star, maybe to use it for a excercise in the future. Now it's the evening, later I'll be taking a walk to gather all my toughts. Now I'm working again on this site after some months.

I've finishing the entrace course the next week, so I could go back home. But I'll be staying there for a week, then I have to go to travel back to start stuying the degree.
