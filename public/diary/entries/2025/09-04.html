Afternoon beer as a mid-week treat

Thurday, 4th September 2025

Previous

I don't have classes on thurdays. In the morning I tried to boot my laptop, but GRUB just broke again and I had to boot Arch Live iso to an USB, and reinstall GRUB. It seems it's working well now. I even put it a Lain theme, it looks cute.

Later, I had hamburgers for lunch, and then I made some math excercises. My friend needed to buy something at the centre, so around 5'clock we took the bus there. I bought an USB to have it flashed with Arch, just in case of an emergency like this morning's. The afternoon was great, there was a fresh brezee, but the sun felt good. There were so many people in the centre, so I wanted to go drink something there. We went to a bar and I ordered black beer, it's been quite a while since I drinked it. Having some beer in the afternoon just makes me feel like I'm travelling with my uncles; I use to share a drink with my uncle, no matter what time it is. So it made cheer up a lot. I think that I should take the most of my days off, and not spend them studying or staying home all day.

I started playing GG Aleste 3, it's a cool game. I tried the switch version on an emulator, but it's incredible that a shmup like that was also realeased as a rom for the Game Gear in 2020.

There's not much else to report. I just felt like writing because these little things made me feel better :)
