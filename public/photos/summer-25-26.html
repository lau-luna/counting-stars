Summer '25 ->'26

Pure random shit captured during my summer vacations from 2025 to 2026. Decided to upload them separated from the Villa Gesell album, since these ones are a bit more boring, compared to the beach and stuff... Regular takes, but I had some fun.

Fun fact: I've almost got detained during these. José took my camera and started taking photos at a house with the flash on from inside the car, that shit is like a flashbang. The house owner came out asking what the heck we were doing at midnight taking pics, he got pretty angry and started calling the cops - Honestly, I would do the same... Just in the middle of the call, my other friend comes out of the house and doesn't understand anything. The house owner was my friend's uncle, and he lived just next to him. Historical save... My friend was like: "I leave you for two minutes and you're already getting detained"; 6th was his reaction. We're sorry...
